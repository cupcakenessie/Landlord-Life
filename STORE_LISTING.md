# LANDLORD LIFE — Google Play Store Listing

## App name
LANDLORD LIFE

## Short description
Build homes, manage tenants, grow businesses and become a City Tycoon!

## Full description
Start with one small rental home and build a property empire from the ground up! LANDLORD LIFE is a colorful mobile management and life simulation game where every decision shapes your growing business.

🏡 BUILD YOUR PROPERTY EMPIRE
Build, furnish, upgrade and expand homes. Improve your properties, raise their value and manage your rental income.

👤 MANAGE REAL TENANTS
Recruit tenants with different personalities, jobs and lifestyles. Respond to requests, handle unexpected events and keep satisfaction high.

💰 MASTER YOUR MONEY
Collect rent, pay maintenance, upgrade furniture and make smart investment decisions. Earn Game Cash through normal gameplay and keep Diamonds for optional premium conveniences.

🌾 GROW YOUR FARM
Unlock crops, animals, workers, managers and changing market opportunities.

🏪 EXPAND INTO BUSINESS
Build shops, businesses and eventually a hotel, beauty spa and school.

🌆 BECOME A CITY TYCOON
Watch your neighborhood grow into a lively city with news, events, services and new opportunities.

🎯 COMPLETE MISSIONS
Earn XP, level up and unlock new parts of your empire.

🎁 PLAY OFFLINE
Core gameplay is designed to remain playable without an internet connection. Optional online features can be added later without making the basic game unusable offline.

LANDLORD LIFE is built to grow for years with new properties, events, businesses, characters and city content.
