LANDLORD LIFE – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

LANDLORD LIFE is a mobile management/life simulation game. The game is designed to support offline play and local game saves.

Information stored locally

Gameplay information such as game progress, currencies, properties, tenants, upgrades and settings may be stored locally on your device so the game can function offline.

Online services

Future versions may offer optional accounts, cloud saves, leaderboards, events, advertising and in-app purchases. These services will only collect information necessary to provide the selected feature and will use the relevant platform/service privacy controls.

Children

The game is not designed to request unnecessary personal information from children. Do not submit personal information through free-text fields.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer using the contact information shown on the official Google Play listing.

Changes

This policy may be updated when new online features are introduced. The latest version will be published with the game.
