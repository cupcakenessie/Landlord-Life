# LANDLORD LIFE — Play Store Release Checklist

1. Create/verify a Google Play Console developer account.
2. Verify the Android device in Play Console when Google requests it.
3. Host the web game and privacy policy on a stable HTTPS domain.
4. Use the PWA manifest and icons included in this package.
5. Package the PWA as an Android App Bundle using a trusted web-to-Android packaging workflow (for example PWABuilder/Bubblewrap) and configure the final Android project to target API 36 or higher.
6. Create the Play Console app as LANDLORD LIFE, Game, free.
7. Complete app access, ads, target audience, content rating, data safety, privacy policy and store listing forms accurately.
8. Upload the signed Android App Bundle to Internal testing first.
9. Test on several Android screen sizes and at least one low-end device.
10. Create a Closed testing track. For a new personal developer account created after Nov 13, 2023, Google requires at least 12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days before production access.
11. Collect tester feedback, fix issues, and apply for Production access.
12. Prepare final screenshots, feature graphic, app icon and release notes.
13. Roll out to Production after Google grants production access.

Important: Google Play currently requires new apps and updates submitted from Aug 31, 2026 to target Android 16/API 36 or higher. Confirm the current requirement at submission time.
